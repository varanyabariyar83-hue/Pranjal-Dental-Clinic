Dental Implants in Greater Noida

A fixed, natural-looking replacement for missing teeth.

About this treatment

A dental implant replaces the root of a missing tooth with a small titanium post placed in the jawbone. Once the bone has healed around it, a crown is attached on top. The result is a tooth that looks natural, stays firmly in place and does not rely on neighbouring teeth for support.

When you may need it

  • You have lost one or more teeth to decay, gum disease or injury
  • Your dentures feel loose or uncomfortable
  • You avoid certain foods because of gaps
  • You want to avoid grinding down healthy teeth for a bridge
  • You are worried about the jawbone shrinking where a tooth is missing

How treatment works

  1. Consultation and scanWe examine your mouth, review your health history and use X-rays or a scan to check bone quality and position.
  2. Treatment planYou receive a clear plan, the number of visits expected and an estimate before anything starts.
  3. Placing the implantUnder local anaesthesia the implant is placed in the jawbone. Some cases need bone support (grafting) first.
  4. HealingThe bone grows around the implant over the following months. A temporary solution can be arranged where suitable.
  5. Crown fittingA custom crown is attached and the bite is checked so it feels natural.
What it feels like. Implant placement is done under local anaesthesia. Some swelling and soreness for a few days is normal and usually settles with the care instructions we give you. Healing time differs from person to person, so we plan around your jaw and your health rather than a fixed calendar.

Aftercare and tips

  • Keep the area clean; brush gently and follow any rinse we advise
  • Avoid smoking, which slows healing and puts implants at risk
  • Eat soft food at first and avoid chewing hard on the new area
  • Attend your review visits so we can check healing
  • After the crown, clean around it daily just like a natural tooth

What is a dental implant?

A dental implant is a small post, usually made of titanium, that is placed in the jawbone where a tooth used to be. Over a few months the bone grows around it and holds it firmly in place. A crown is then attached on top, so the finished tooth looks and feels natural and does not rely on neighbouring teeth for support.

For people searching for dental implants in Greater Noida or tooth replacement near me, this is the main appeal: a fixed solution that does not slip like a loose denture and does not require healthy teeth to be filed down, as a traditional bridge does.

Who is suitable for an implant?

Most healthy adults with enough jawbone and healthy gums can have implants. We check your general health, your gums and the bone itself before recommending treatment. Habits and conditions that slow healing, such as smoking or poorly controlled diabetes, are discussed honestly, since they affect how well an implant settles.

If the bone has thinned after a long period without a tooth, a bone-support procedure may be suggested first. After your scan we will tell you plainly whether an implant is right for you or whether another option would suit you better.

Implants, bridges and dentures compared

There are three common ways to replace missing teeth. An implant replaces the tooth root and the crown, protects the bone and stands on its own. A bridge uses the neighbouring teeth as anchors, which can be quicker but means those teeth are shaped to carry the bridge. A denture is removable and often the most economical, but it can feel less stable and does not stimulate the jawbone.

Each option has its place, and the best choice depends on your teeth, your bone, your budget and your preferences. Our job is to explain the trade-offs clearly and let you decide.

Moving teeth before an implant

Sometimes the gap left by a missing tooth has narrowed because neighbouring teeth have tilted into it. In such cases, braces or clear aligners can open up the space so that an implant crown can sit comfortably. Planning orthodontic treatment and implant work together often gives a better, longer-lasting result than treating either in isolation.

Caring for your implant

Implants cannot decay, but the gums around them can still become inflamed if plaque is left behind. Brush twice a day, clean around the crown and under it with interdental brushes or floss, avoid smoking and attend your review visits. Looked after well, an implant can serve you for many years.

How long does implant treatment take?

The timeline depends on your bone and healing. Planning and scans come first, followed by placement of the implant, a healing period while the bone integrates, and finally the crown. Some people need extra steps such as bone support. We will give you a realistic outline at your consultation rather than a fixed promise.

Common questions

Who is suitable for an implant?

Most healthy adults with enough jawbone and healthy gums. Conditions such as uncontrolled diabetes or heavy smoking can affect healing, so we check your health first.

How long do implants last?

With good cleaning and regular check-ups they can last many years. Gum health is the main factor.

What if I do not have enough bone?

Bone support procedures are sometimes possible. We will tell you honestly after the scan whether an implant or another option suits you best.

Will people notice that I have an implant?

A well-planned implant crown is shaped and shaded to match your natural teeth, so most people will not be able to tell which tooth is the implant.

Can several missing teeth be replaced with implants?

Yes. Depending on the situation, one implant can support a single crown, or a few implants can support a bridge or a set of teeth. We plan this after a scan.

How do I clean a dental implant?

Brush it as you would a natural tooth and clean around the edges of the crown with floss or interdental brushes. We will demonstrate the right technique.

Implant or bridge: which is better?

An implant avoids reshaping healthy neighbouring teeth and helps protect the bone, while a bridge can be faster. We will compare both for your mouth and explain the cost of each.
