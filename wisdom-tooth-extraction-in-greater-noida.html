Wisdom Tooth Extraction in Greater Noida

Safe removal of painful or impacted wisdom teeth.

About this treatment

Wisdom teeth are the last molars to appear, usually in the late teens or twenties. When there is not enough room, they can come in at an angle, stay stuck under the gum, or trap food and bacteria. Removing a problem wisdom tooth is a routine procedure that stops repeated pain and protects the teeth beside it.

When you may need it

  • Pain or swelling at the very back of the jaw
  • Gum around the tooth that is red, sore or keeps getting infected
  • Food repeatedly getting trapped behind the last tooth
  • Bad taste or odour from the back of the mouth
  • Difficulty opening your mouth fully
  • Damage or decay in the neighbouring tooth

How treatment works

  1. X-ray and planningAn X-ray shows the position of the tooth and its roots so we can plan the safest approach.
  2. NumbingThe area is numbed before we start. You should feel pressure but not pain.
  3. RemovalSome teeth come out easily; impacted ones may need a small opening in the gum or careful sectioning.
  4. Stitches if neededWhere required, stitches help the gum heal neatly.
  5. Aftercare briefingWe give clear written and spoken instructions before you go home.
What it feels like. Most people are surprised by how manageable the procedure feels once the area is numb. Afterwards some swelling, stiffness and soreness for a few days is normal. Plan a quieter day or two so you can rest.

Aftercare and tips

  • Bite on the gauze for the time we advise to help a clot form
  • Do not rinse forcefully, spit or use a straw for the first day
  • Eat soft, cool food and avoid very hot drinks for the first day
  • Use a cold pack on the cheek for the first day, then warm salt-water rinses as advised
  • Avoid smoking while the socket heals
  • Call us if pain gets worse after the first few days, or if swelling or fever increases

Wisdom tooth removal in Greater Noida

Wisdom teeth are the last molars to come through, usually between the late teens and the twenties. Many people have enough room for them and never have a problem. Others find that these teeth come in at an angle, stay trapped under the gum or trap food and bacteria, leading to repeated pain and swelling. If you have been searching for wisdom tooth removal in Greater Noida or a wisdom tooth dentist near me, the first step is simply to have the tooth examined and X-rayed.

What is an impacted wisdom tooth?

A wisdom tooth is called impacted when it cannot come through the gum properly because something is blocking its way, often the tooth in front of it or the jawbone itself. An impacted tooth can press on its neighbour, trap food and cause infection of the surrounding gum, known as pericoronitis. Typical signs are pain at the back of the jaw, a bad taste, swelling and difficulty opening wide.

When does a wisdom tooth need to be removed?

Not every wisdom tooth needs to go. If it is healthy, fully through the gum and easy to clean, it can stay. We recommend removal when the tooth keeps causing infection, damages the neighbouring tooth, has decay that cannot be restored, or is likely to cause trouble in future. Sometimes removal is also advised before orthodontic treatment. We explain our reasons and show you the X-ray so that the advice is clear.

How the extraction is done

The area is numbed first, so you feel pressure but not pain. A straightforward tooth is loosened and lifted out. An impacted tooth may need a small opening in the gum, and the tooth is sometimes divided into pieces so that it can be removed gently. Stitches are placed where needed. Most of our patients are surprised by how manageable the visit feels.

Recovery and aftercare

Plan for a quieter day or two. Expect some swelling and stiffness that peak over the first couple of days and then ease. Bite on the gauze as advised, avoid rinsing hard or using a straw on the first day, eat soft cool food and keep the mouth clean with gentle salt-water rinses from the next day. If your pain increases after the third day, or you notice fever or spreading swelling, please call us promptly.

Common questions

Does every wisdom tooth need to be removed?

No. If a wisdom tooth is healthy, fully erupted and easy to clean, it can stay. We recommend removal only when it causes or is likely to cause problems.

How long is recovery?

Many people feel much better within a few days, though full healing of the gum takes longer. We will give you a plan for your own case.

Can I go to work the next day?

Often yes for desk work, but it depends on how difficult the extraction was. Ask us at the visit.

Is wisdom tooth removal painful?

The area is fully numbed, so you should feel pressure rather than pain during the procedure. Soreness and swelling afterwards are normal and manageable with the aftercare we explain.

Can all four wisdom teeth be removed together?

Sometimes yes, sometimes they are done in separate visits. It depends on their position and your comfort. We will advise after an X-ray.

What can I eat after wisdom tooth extraction?

Soft, cool food such as curd, khichdi, mashed vegetables and soup (not hot) for the first day or two, avoiding spicy and crunchy items until the area heals.
