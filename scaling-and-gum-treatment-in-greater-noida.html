Scaling & Gum Treatment in Greater Noida

Professional cleaning and care for bleeding, swollen or receding gums.

About this treatment

Healthy gums are the foundation of healthy teeth. Plaque that is not brushed away hardens into tartar, which only a professional can remove. Left alone it irritates the gums, causing bleeding and bad breath, and can eventually damage the bone that holds teeth in place.

When you may need it

  • Gums that bleed when you brush or floss
  • Red, puffy or tender gums
  • Persistent bad breath
  • Gums pulling away from the teeth
  • Yellow or brown build-up near the gum line
  • Teeth that feel loose

How treatment works

  1. Gum examinationWe check your gums, measure any pockets around the teeth and take X-rays if required.
  2. ScalingTartar and plaque are removed above and below the gum line.
  3. PolishingTeeth are polished to remove stains and make it harder for plaque to stick.
  4. Deeper cleaning if neededIf gum disease has gone deeper, we plan more detailed cleaning of the roots.
  5. Home care planWe show you the right brushing and flossing technique for your mouth.
What it feels like. Scaling is usually well tolerated. If your gums are very tender we can numb the area. Light bleeding and some sensitivity in the first few days is common, and gums often look healthier within a short time as they heal.

Aftercare and tips

  • Brush gently twice a day with a soft brush, angling toward the gum line
  • Clean between your teeth daily with floss or interdental brushes
  • Use any mouthwash only as advised
  • Avoid tobacco, which makes gum disease worse
  • Come for professional cleaning at the interval we recommend, commonly every six months

What is dental scaling?

Scaling is a professional cleaning in which the dentist removes plaque and tartar from your teeth, above and below the gum line, using specialised dental instruments. It is followed by polishing, which smooths the tooth surface and removes stains. Many people feel that their mouth is fresher and their teeth look brighter afterwards. Scaling does not damage the teeth. The gaps you may notice afterwards were filled with tartar before.

Signs of gum disease

Bleeding when you brush or floss, red or swollen gums, persistent bad breath, gums that are pulling away from your teeth, pus between teeth and teeth that feel loose are all warning signs. Early gum disease, called gingivitis, can often be reversed with cleaning and good home care. If it advances to periodontitis, the bone supporting the teeth is affected, and treatment aims to control the disease and prevent further loss.

Deep cleaning and ongoing gum care

When gum disease has progressed, a standard scale may not be enough. Deeper cleaning of the root surfaces below the gum is then recommended, sometimes over more than one visit, followed by regular reviews. We examine your gums, measure any pockets around the teeth, explain what we find and plan treatment that matches the problem.

Bad breath and bleeding gums

Persistent bad breath most often starts in the mouth, from plaque, tartar, a coated tongue or gum infection. Treating the cause with a thorough clean, gum care and better home habits usually helps. Bleeding gums should never be ignored or brushed around, since it is a sign of inflammation that needs attention. If bad breath continues despite a clean mouth, we may suggest that you see your physician.

How often should you get your teeth cleaned?

Most people benefit from a professional clean every six months, though some need it more often, such as people who form tartar quickly, smokers, people with diabetes and anyone with a history of gum disease. Common questions

Does scaling weaken the teeth?

No. It removes the deposits that damage the gums. Gaps you notice afterwards were previously filled with tartar.

Why do my gums bleed?

Usually because of inflammation caused by plaque. Healthy gums should not bleed, so it is a sign to get them checked.

Can gum disease be reversed?

Early gum inflammation can often be reversed with cleaning and good home care. More advanced gum disease can be controlled, which is why early treatment matters.

Does scaling hurt?

Usually it is well tolerated. If your gums are tender, we can numb the area to keep you comfortable.

Will my teeth become loose after scaling?

No. Scaling removes the tartar that was irritating the gums. If teeth feel looser, it is because inflammation was hiding gaps caused by existing gum disease, and your dentist will advise on treatment.

Why do my gums bleed when I brush?

It is usually due to inflammation from plaque. Healthy gums should not bleed, so it is a sign to have them checked and cleaned.
