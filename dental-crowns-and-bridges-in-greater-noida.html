Dental Crowns & Bridges in Greater Noida

Strong, natural-looking restorations for damaged or missing teeth.

About this treatment

A crown is a cap that fits over a tooth to restore its shape, strength and appearance. A bridge uses crowns on neighbouring teeth to hold an artificial tooth in the gap. Both are used when a filling is not enough or when a tooth has been lost.

When you may need it

  • A large filling that keeps breaking
  • A cracked or weakened tooth
  • A tooth that has had a root canal
  • Worn-down or badly discoloured teeth
  • A gap where a tooth is missing and neighbours are healthy

How treatment works

  1. ExaminationWe check the tooth, the gum and the bite and take X-rays where needed.
  2. Preparing the toothUnder local anaesthesia the tooth is shaped to make room for the crown.
  3. Impression or scanAn exact record of your teeth is taken so the crown fits properly.
  4. Temporary crownA temporary crown protects the tooth while the final one is made.
  5. FittingThe permanent crown or bridge is checked for colour, fit and bite, then cemented in place.
What it feels like. Treatment is done under local anaesthesia. Teeth can be slightly sensitive for a few days after preparation. Materials differ in strength, look and cost; your dentist will explain which are suitable for your tooth and your budget.

Aftercare and tips

  • Clean carefully around the crown edge and under a bridge using floss threaders or interdental brushes
  • Avoid biting very hard objects such as ice or pen caps
  • Attend regular check-ups so we can check the margins and gums
  • Let us know if the bite feels high or the crown loosens

Dental crowns and bridges in Greater Noida

A tooth that has been badly broken, heavily filled or treated with a root canal often needs more than a simple filling. A dental crown, sometimes called a tooth cap, covers the tooth completely to restore its shape, strength and appearance. A dental bridge replaces a missing tooth by joining an artificial tooth to crowns on the neighbouring teeth. If you have been searching for dental crowns in Greater Noida or a tooth cap near me, this page explains how they work.

When is a crown recommended?

We suggest a crown when a tooth has lost too much of its structure to be repaired with a filling, after a root canal, when a tooth is cracked or worn, to protect a weak tooth from breaking, or to improve the look of a severely discoloured or misshapen tooth. A crown protects what remains of the tooth and spreads the chewing load, so that a repaired tooth can keep working for years.

Crown materials and appearance

Crowns can be made from several materials, including metal-ceramic, all-ceramic and zirconia. They differ in strength, appearance and cost. Back teeth that take a heavy bite may suit stronger materials, while front teeth are usually made with tooth-coloured materials that match the shade of your smile. We explain the options for your tooth and your budget, and shade-match the crown carefully.

How a dental bridge works

A bridge fills the gap left by a missing tooth. The teeth on either side of the gap are shaped to take crowns, and the replacement tooth is fixed between them. It is a fixed solution that you do not take out, and it usually takes fewer visits than an implant. The trade-off is that healthy neighbouring teeth have to be reduced. Implants, bridges and dentures are compared on our dental implants page so that you can decide with confidence.

Looking after your crown or bridge

Crowns and bridges need the same care as natural teeth. Brush twice a day and clean carefully along the gum line. Under a bridge, use floss threaders or interdental brushes to remove plaque. Avoid biting hard objects such as ice, pen caps or nuts with a crown. Regular check-ups allow us to catch any loosening or gum problem early.

Common questions

How long does a crown take?

It normally involves at least two visits: one to prepare the tooth and one to fit the crown. Your dentist will confirm for your case.

Will it look natural?

Tooth-coloured materials can be matched to the shade of your other teeth.

Crown, bridge or implant for a missing tooth?

Each has advantages. We will explain the pros and cons for your mouth so you can choose with confidence.

What is the difference between a crown and a cap?

They are the same thing. Dentists call it a crown, while many patients say tooth cap.

How long do dental crowns last?

With good cleaning, regular check-ups and sensible habits, crowns can last for many years. Their lifespan also depends on the material and the health of the underlying tooth.

Is getting a crown painful?

The tooth is numbed before it is prepared, so the procedure itself should feel comfortable. Mild sensitivity for a few days is possible.
