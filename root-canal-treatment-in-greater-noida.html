Root Canal Treatment in Greater Noida

Save a badly infected or painful tooth instead of losing it.

About this treatment

A root canal is the treatment that lets you keep a tooth whose inner pulp has become infected or badly inflamed. Instead of removing the tooth, the dentist cleans out the damaged tissue, seals the space, and then protects the tooth with a filling or crown. At Pranjal Dental Clinic in Jagat Farm we explain each stage before we begin, so you always know what is happening.

When you may need it

  • A toothache that lingers after hot or cold drinks
  • Throbbing pain that disturbs your sleep
  • Pain when you bite or press on the tooth
  • A swelling or small pimple on the gum near one tooth
  • A tooth that has turned darker than its neighbours
  • Deep decay or a crack that reaches the inner part of the tooth

How treatment works

  1. Examination and X-rayWe listen to your symptoms, test the tooth and take an X-ray to see how far the infection has spread.
  2. NumbingThe area is made numb so the treatment itself is comfortable. Tell us at any point if you need a pause.
  3. Cleaning the canalsThe infected pulp is removed and the inside of the tooth is carefully cleaned and shaped.
  4. SealingThe cleaned canals are filled and sealed so bacteria cannot return.
  5. Final protectionA filling or crown is placed. A root-treated tooth can become brittle, so this last step matters.
What it feels like. With proper numbing most patients feel pressure and vibration rather than pain. The pain people associate with root canals usually comes from the infection itself, and treatment is what relieves it. Depending on the tooth and the infection, treatment may take one visit or more; we will tell you which after the examination.

Aftercare and tips

  • Mild tenderness for a few days is common; take any medicine only as advised by your dentist
  • Avoid chewing hard food on that tooth until the final filling or crown is done
  • Do not skip the crown or final restoration if we recommend one
  • Brush gently twice a day and floss as usual
  • Call us if swelling, fever or strong pain appears or returns

Root canal or extraction: which is better?

Where it is possible, keeping your natural tooth is usually the better choice. A natural tooth holds its place in the jaw, helps you chew properly and keeps neighbouring teeth from drifting. Removing a tooth leaves a gap that later needs a bridge or an implant, which means more visits and more expense.

Extraction becomes the sensible option when a tooth is cracked below the gum line, has too little healthy structure left to rebuild, or is badly loosened by gum disease. We discuss both paths openly so the decision is yours, made with the full picture.

Why a crown is often needed after a root canal

A tooth that has had a root canal no longer has a living pulp, and with time it can become more brittle, particularly at the back of the mouth where chewing forces are strongest. A crown wraps the tooth like a protective cap and spreads those forces evenly, which greatly reduces the risk of a fracture.

Front teeth that are largely intact sometimes manage with a filling alone. Your dentist will recommend what fits your tooth. Whichever it is, please complete the final restoration; a sealed canal with a missing crown is one of the most common reasons treated teeth fail later. You can read more about this on our dental crowns and bridges page.

Root canal treated teeth and braces

Many adults ask whether a tooth that has had a root canal can still be moved with braces or clear aligners. In most cases it can, as long as the tooth is healthy, well sealed and free of infection. We always settle any infection first and review the tooth on an X-ray before starting orthodontic treatment, so that your smile correction rests on a stable foundation.

What affects the cost of root canal treatment

People often search for root canal cost in Greater Noida, and it is a fair question. The honest answer is that the cost depends on the tooth involved, the number and shape of its canals, how severe the infection is, how much rebuilding the tooth needs and the type of crown chosen afterwards. A front tooth with a single canal is a very different job from a lower molar with several.

That is why we give an estimate only after examining you and viewing the X-ray. You will know the plan and the expected cost before we start, and we will tell you if anything changes along the way.

Common questions

Is a root canal painful?

The treatment is done under local anaesthesia, so you should feel pressure rather than pain. The toothache you came in with is usually what the treatment removes.

Will I need a crown afterwards?

Often yes, especially for back teeth that handle heavy chewing. Your dentist will advise based on how much healthy tooth remains.

Can I eat after the visit?

Wait until the numbness wears off, then eat soft food and chew on the opposite side until the tooth is fully restored.

How much does a root canal cost in Greater Noida?

Can a root canal treated tooth get infected again?

It can if the tooth is not sealed and restored properly or if decay returns. A well-done root canal with a proper crown or filling, good cleaning and regular check-ups gives the tooth its best chance of lasting for years.

Is it painful after the root canal is done?

Mild tenderness for a few days is common and usually settles. Severe pain, swelling or fever is not normal, and you should call us the same day.
